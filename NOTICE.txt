High Reward Web Remake
Original game names, story and art belong to their respective rights holders.
High-resolution art is a derivative reconstruction generated from the supplied reference artwork.
Generation does not establish rights ownership or a distribution license.
This site contains the native JavaScript reconstruction and no DOS executables or game archives.
Original campaign rules execute through a restricted JavaScript instruction model; battle rules have an independently tested native port.
Browser interface, redraws, timing and synthesized audio are presentation adaptations.
Differential and UI checks cover documented cases, not every possible campaign history.
